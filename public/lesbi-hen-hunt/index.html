How it works

  • Split into 3 teams of 3. You have ~2 hours.
  • Find items, take photos as proof — you don't have to buy them (except the final challenge).
  • Earn +1 bonus point on ANY challenge for a creative, funny, or extra-fabulous photo.
  • Tally scores at the end. Sarah judges the final challenge.
  • Disputes settled by vibes.
